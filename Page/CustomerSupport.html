Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
QuestionAnswer
How do I cancel a ticket?Go to "My Tickets", find your booking, and click the "Cancel" button. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
Can I change my seat after booking?Yes, you can change your seat up to 2 hours before departure, subject to availability, via the dashboard.
What is the luggage policy?Each passenger can carry up to 20kg of luggage for free. Additional luggage will be charged separately.
How long does a refund take?Refunds are typically processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method.
